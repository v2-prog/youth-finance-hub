# Companion Spreadsheet Outline: Contribution & Age Milestones

**Educational tool only. Not personal advice. Update with current ATO figures.**

## Suggested Sheets / Tabs

1. **Age Band Overview**
   - Columns: Age Band | Key Milestones | SG Eligible | Contribution Options | Access Rules | Special Schemes

2. **SG Accumulation Examples**
   - Income levels: $60k, $90k, $120k, $180k
   - Years of contributions at 12% SG
   - Projected balance at 60 / 67 (assume 6–7% net return for illustration)
   - With and without salary sacrifice

3. **Concessional Cap Tracker**
   - Current cap $32,500 (2026–27)
   - Carry-forward eligibility (TSB < $500k)
   - Personal deductible vs salary sacrifice

4. **Non-Concessional & Bring-Forward**
   - $130k annual / up to $390k bring-forward
   - TSB thresholds for full/partial/nil

5. **FHSSS Tracker**
   - Annual $15k eligible
   - Lifetime $50k releasable + earnings
   - Release timing rules

6. **Downsizer Planner**
   - Age 55+
   - Up to $300k each
   - 10-year ownership + main residence rules
   - Timing within 90 days of settlement

7. **Wealth Level Projections**
   - Modest / Comfortable / Wealthy columns
   - Super + other assets at key ages (30, 40, 50, 60, 67)
   - ASFA comparison (comfortable $56k single / $79k couple)

8. **Family of Five Cashflow**
   - Dual income examples
   - Education / childcare costs by child age
   - Contribution capacity remaining

9. **SMSF / Property Notes**
   - Self-management considerations
   - Property in SMSF rules (limited)
   - Debt recycling illustration

10. **Eco-Village / Cooperative Funding**
    - Grant / DGR / community contribution ideas
    - Structure Lab continuity checklist

## Usage Notes
- Use simple formulas for compound growth.
- Flag that projections are illustrative only.
- Include disclaimer row on every sheet.
- Colour-code bands: early (green), accumulation (amber), transition (blue), retirement (purple).

This outline can be built in Excel/Google Sheets and linked from the coeconomics.com planner pages.
