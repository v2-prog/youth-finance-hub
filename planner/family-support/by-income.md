# Family Support by household income context

**General information only. Verify current rules on official sites. Not personal advice.** Last verified 2026-11-03.

## How the bands are defined
Source: ABS, *Household Income and Wealth, Australia, 2019–20* (latest release, published 2022) — https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/finance/household-income-and-wealth-australia/latest-release
Equivalised disposable household income (EDHI), per week, 2019–20:

| Band | ABS figure (2019–20) |
|---|---|
| Lowest 10% | at or below P10: **$475/week** |
| Average | mean **$1,124/week** (median $958) |
| Highest 10% | at or above P90: **$1,903/week** |

⚠️ **Data older than 3 years.** This is the latest ABS Survey of Income and Housing release found; real incomes have changed since. ABS *Measuring What Matters* reports a 2022-23 median of $1,192/week (June 2023 prices, HILDA-based) but no P10/P90 — https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/measuring-what-matters/measuring-what-matters-themes-and-indicators/prosperous/household-income-and-wealth
EDHI is equivalised (OECD-modified scale: 1 for first adult, 0.5 per extra person 15+, 0.3 per child under 15), so it is **not** directly comparable with the family adjusted-taxable-income tests below. Bands are context only; eligibility is always set by each program's own test.
