# Ages 40–41: Peak Accumulation & Family

**General information only. Verify current rules on official sites. Not personal advice.**

## Life Events
- Higher income years for many.
- Children in school or high school (education costs).
- Mortgage often substantial.
- Opportunity for carry-forward concessional contributions.

## Scenarios

### Scenario A: Family of five (dual income)
Two incomes, three children of varying ages. Child Care Subsidy / Family Tax Benefit relevant earlier; now education and extracurricular costs. Debt recycling or salary sacrifice prioritised.

### Scenario B: Property portfolio + SMSF planning
Multiple properties. Considering SMSF for control (self-management from ~2027 planning). W-8BEN-E for US exposure if relevant. Debt recycling into deductible debt.

### Scenario C: Eco-village / cooperative funding
Exploring CLT, cooperative housing, or land trust models. Using Structure Lab continuity frameworks for governance, succession, and circular economy funding (grants, DGR status, community coupons).

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Large family home vs multi-generational.
- Regional acreage for eco-build.
- Cooperative or cohousing with shared facilities.
- Investment properties + smaller primary residence.

## Wealth Level Snapshot (couple, end of band)

| Level | Combined Super | Other Assets | Cashflow Notes | Age Pension Path |
|-------|----------------|--------------|----------------|------------------|
| Modest | $200–400k | Home equity low or renting | Tight with family costs | Likely full/partial later |
| Comfortable | $400–800k | Solid home equity + some investments | On track for ASFA comfortable | Partial Age Pension likely |
| Wealthy | > $1.5M | Multiple properties + investments | Strong surplus | Self-funded or asset-tested |

## Structure Lab Elements
- Household Continuity Planner for family governance.
- Succession planning for any business or property portfolio.
- Cooperative models for shared land or eco-village.

## Key Actions
- Use carry-forward unused concessional caps if TSB < $500k.
- Review estate planning (testamentary trusts).
- Model retirement projections against ASFA $56k/$79k targets.
- Insurance review for dependents.

Sources: ATO, ASFA, Services Australia, Moneysmart.
