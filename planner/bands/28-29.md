# Ages 28–29: First Home & Wealth Building

**General information only. Verify current rules on official sites. Not personal advice.**

## Life Events
- Career establishment, possible first home purchase.
- FHSSS release eligible if contributions made.
- Possible start of family or dual income household.
- Super balance growing with 12% SG + any voluntary.

## Scenarios

### Scenario A: First home buyer (urban)
Using FHSSS + First Home Guarantee or state schemes. Mortgage starts. Focus on offset account.

### Scenario B: Investing outside super
Shares/ETFs or small investment property. Negative gearing considerations. Keep liquid emergency fund.

### Scenario C: Regional or cohousing
Lower purchase price, longer commute or cooperative living. Potential for earlier equity build or community structures.

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Ownership with mortgage vs continued renting.
- Multi-generational home purchase.
- Eco-village or cooperative land share (Structure Lab land & purpose models).
- Regional lifestyle with remote work.

## Wealth Level Snapshot (single, end of band)

| Level | Super | Property Equity / Investments | Annual Potential | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------------------------------|------------------|-------|
| Modest | $40–80k | Renting or minimal equity | Building | Catch-up needed |
| Comfortable | $80–150k | Some equity or ETFs | On track for ASFA | Salary sacrifice helping |
| Wealthy track | > $150k | Significant equity + investments | Ahead | High income + early contributions |

## Key Actions
- Maximise concessional contributions within $32,500 cap.
- Review insurance needs if dependents.
- Update will and super nominations if partnering.
- Track FHSSS if still relevant.

Sources: ATO FHSSS, Housing Australia, state revenue offices, Moneysmart.
