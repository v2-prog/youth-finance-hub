# Ages 18–19: Launch into Work & Super

**General information only. Verify current rules on official sites. Not personal advice.**

## Life Events & Milestones
- First full-time or significant part-time job (SG 12% starts).
- TFN registration and linking to super.
- Possible start of HELP/HECS if studying.
- Moving out or living with parents (ABS data historically shows high share of 20–24s still at home).

## Scenarios

### Scenario A: Starting work while studying
Casual/part-time income ~$25–40k. SG contributions small but start compounding. Focus on emergency fund and avoiding high-interest debt.

### Scenario B: Full-time apprenticeship or trade
Steady income, earlier super accumulation. Opportunity for salary sacrifice once income rises.

### Scenario C: Gap year / travel then study
Low or irregular income. Prioritise TFN, basic super account, and financial literacy.

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Living at home (lower costs, higher savings rate).
- Share house (common in capital cities).
- Regional town for lower rent while studying or working.
- Multi-generational support.

## Wealth Level Snapshot (end of band, single)

| Level | Super (approx) | Other Assets | Notes |
|-------|----------------|--------------|-------|
| Modest | $2–8k | Minimal | Early SG only |
| Comfortable track | $8–15k | Small savings | Higher hours or salary sacrifice start |
| Ahead | > $15k | Personal savings + early investments | Strong habits or family support |

## Key Actions
- Choose or consolidate super fund (check fees, insurance).
- Open TFN if needed and link accounts.
- Start FHSSS voluntary contributions if first-home goal exists.
- Build basic budget and emergency buffer.

Official sources: [ATO Super](https://www.ato.gov.au), [Moneysmart](https://moneysmart.gov.au), [Services Australia](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au).
