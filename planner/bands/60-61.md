# Ages 60–61: Transition to Retirement Access

**General information only. Verify current rules on official sites. Not personal advice.**

## Life Events
- Preservation age reached. Access to super if retired or after ceasing employment.
- TTR income stream available while still working (4–10% drawdown).
- Downsizer contributions possible from age 55.
- Planning for Age Pension at 67.

## Scenarios

### Scenario A: Still working + TTR
Salary sacrifice + TTR drawdown for tax efficiency. Builds tax-free component.

### Scenario B: Property + SMSF self-managed
SMSF in place or transitioning. Property in or outside SMSF. Downsizer from home sale if eligible. Control over investments including international.

### Scenario C: Eco-village / cooperative retirement
Moving to or funding community land / cohousing. Structure Lab continuity and governance models for shared assets and succession.

## Alternative Living Arrangements
- Downsizing main residence (downsizer contribution up to $300k each).
- Multi-generational with adult children.
- Regional or eco-village living.
- Rental in retirement (higher ASFA cost; more super needed).

## Wealth Level Snapshot (single, end of band)

| Level | Super | Other Assets | Drawdown Potential | Age Pension at 67 |
|-------|-------|--------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Modest | < $250k | Limited equity | Age Pension dominant | Full or high partial |
| Comfortable | $500–800k | Home equity + investments | ASFA comfortable range | Partial |
| Wealthy | > $1.5M | Significant property + investments | Self-funded | Unlikely |

## Key Actions
- Decide on retirement timing and TTR strategy.
- Review Transfer Balance Cap ($2.1M) planning.
- Update binding death benefit nominations.
- Model Age Pension means tests.

Sources: ATO (super access, downsizer, TTR), Services Australia (Age Pension), ASFA, Moneysmart.
