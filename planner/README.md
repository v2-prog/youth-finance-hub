### Wealth Level Tables (template used in bands)

Tables show approximate positions at the end of the age band for a single person (adjust for couples). Figures are illustrative, based on ASFA June 2026 standards and 2026 super rules. Outcomes depend heavily on income history, contributions, property, and investment returns.

| Wealth Level | Super Balance (approx) | Other Assets / Equity | Annual Cashflow Potential | Age Pension Indicator (at 67) | Lifestyle Notes |
|--------------|------------------------|-----------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------|
| Slight poverty / Modest | < $150k | Minimal or renting | Relies on Age Pension + part-time work | Full or near-full | ASFA modest ~$36.5k single (homeowner); tighter if renting |
| Comfortable | $400k–$700k | Some shares/ETFs or small property equity | Super drawdown + part Age Pension | Partial | ASFA comfortable ~$56k single |
| Wealthy | > $1.5M | Significant property equity + investments | Fully self-funded or high drawdown | Unlikely or asset-tested out | Above ASFA; travel, private health, gifting capacity |
