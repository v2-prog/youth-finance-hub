# Scenario: Property Portfolio + SMSF Self-Management

**Educational only. Not personal or SMSF advice. Seek licensed advice.**

## Overview
Common for accumulators in 40s–50s with multiple properties and desire for control over investments (including international exposure via W-8BEN-E).

## Key Elements
- Existing property equity used for debt recycling or further investment.
- SMSF established or planned (self-management transition).
- Concessional contributions maximised; carry-forward used.
- Downsizer available from 55 if main residence sold.
- Estate planning with binding nominations and possible testamentary trust.

## Structure Lab Alignment
- Household Continuity Planner for succession of property and SMSF trustee roles.
- Clear governance for family members as directors/trustees.
- Purpose structures if part of eco or community land goals.
