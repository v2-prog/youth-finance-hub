# Scenario: Eco-Village or Cooperative Living Funding

**Educational only. Not personal, legal or tax advice.**

## Models
- Community Land Trust (CLT).
- Cooperative housing or cohousing.
- Eco-village with shared infrastructure (renewables, food, governance).
- First Nations Land Trust style or Quaker-inspired cooperative.

## Funding Pathways
- Personal super and property equity.
- Grants, subsidies, R&D or farming grants (DGR charities/NGOs).
- Community contributions or local currency/coupon systems (Structure Lab circular economy ideas).
- Shared equity or cooperative membership fees.

## Governance (Structure Lab)
- Cooperative Continuity Planner.
- SOPs, team roles, succession.
- Binding arrangements for land and purpose structures.
- Integration with individual household planning.

## Living Arrangement Notes
- Lower individual housing cost.
- Shared decision-making.
- Potential for multi-generational and intergenerational wealth within the community.
- Rural/regional preference for acreage and ecological living.

## Wealth Implications
- Capital may be locked in community structures.
- Liquidity planning essential.
- Super remains personal; community assets separate.
- Age Pension means testing may treat community interests carefully — seek advice.

Sources: BCCM, ACNC, state land and housing authorities, Moneysmart.
