# Scenario: Family of Five

**Educational only. Not personal advice.**

## Typical Profile (ages 35–50)
- Dual income household.
- Three children (e.g. primary, high school, and younger).
- Mortgage on family home.
- Education, activities, and possibly childcare costs.
- Super contributions often constrained by cashflow but high priority once capacity returns.

## Cashflow Considerations
- Family Tax Benefit and Child Care Subsidy (earlier years).
- Private school or extracurricular fees.
- Offset account on home loan.
- Salary sacrifice when possible.

## Living Arrangements
- Large suburban home.
- Multi-generational if extended family nearby.
- Regional for lower housing cost and lifestyle.
- Cooperative or eco-village for shared resources.

## Wealth Snapshot (couple, mid-40s)

| Level | Combined Super | Home Equity | Notes |
|-------|----------------|-------------|-------|
| Modest | $250–450k | Building | Focus on debt reduction + contributions |
| Comfortable | $500–900k | Solid | ASFA track with part Age Pension later |
| Wealthy | > $1.5M | Strong + investments | Self-funded options |
