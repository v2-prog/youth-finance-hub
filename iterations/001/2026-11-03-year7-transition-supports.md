# Year 7 transition supports (12–13 band) — ACT first

Iteration 001 · 2026-11-03 · Bracket – Two years at a time (CoEconomics)
Audience: families moving from Year 6 into Year 7 in the ACT; other states as comparisons only.
All items last verified **2026-11-03** unless marked otherwise. Figures are quoted as stated on the official page; none are calculated or estimated.

## Summary
- Turning 13 changes Commonwealth family payments: FTB Part A maximum rises; FTB Part B stops for couples (continues for single parents/grandparent carers).
- ACT public schools give every Year 7 student a Chromebook; BYOD is also allowed.
- Year 7 school vaccinations (HPV, dTpa) run Terms 3–4 with a consent card that must be returned even when declining.
- Canberra household supports useful to Year 7 families: Electricity, Gas and Water Rebate ($800, formerly "Utilities Concession"), Home Energy Support rebates, Sustainable Household Scheme loans (now 3%, not interest-free, for most households), Future of Education Equity Fund for school costs.
- Name checks: "Bright loans" = **Brighte**, the private lender partnered with the ACT Government to deliver Sustainable Household Scheme loans; there is no ACT "Bright Start"/"Bright loan grant" home-buyer scheme found on official sites. "e-smart" was not found; the likely matches are the ACT Government's **Actsmart** program (historic sustainability program; current status needs-checking) and **SEE-Change** (community group running the Canberra Electric Bike Library). No "e-smart" program verified.

## Live-update mechanism (proposal)
- **Review cadence per item:** 1 Jan (CDBS cap), 20 Mar / 20 Sep (Services Australia indexation — FTB, PLP), 1 Jul (financial year, ACT Budget measures, rebate amounts), Term 3 (vaccination consent card), Nov (Equity Fund close, rental insulation deadline 30 Nov 2026).
- **Staleness flag:** each item carries `date_verified` and `next_review_date`; past the review date the item flips to `needs-checking` automatically.
- **Source change detection:** store a content hash of each official page's main text; a monthly routine re-fetches links, compares hashes, checks for 404s, and writes a needs-checking report.
- **On the page:** each support shows "Last verified 3 Nov 2026" and, when stale or flagged, "Check before relying". No user data stored on a server.
