# Health supports for ages 10–15 — ACT/Canberra first

Iteration 002 · 2026-11-03 · Bracket – Two years at a time (CoEconomics)
All items last verified **2026-11-03**. Status: ✅ verified · ⚠️ needs-checking · ⛔ closed/uncertain.
Figures and terms are quoted as stated on the linked page; none are estimated. This is a directory of services, not medical advice — talk to a GP or health professional about any individual child.

## Summary
- **Free public options cover most basics in the ACT:** Children's Hearing Service (18 months–18), public child and youth dental (under 14 for all ACT residents/ACT school students; under 18 with a concession card), Year 7 and Year 10 school vaccinations, School Youth Health Nurses in public high schools, headspace (12–25) and CAMHS.
- **Bulk billing:** since 1 Nov 2025 the GP bulk-billing incentive applies to all Medicare-eligible patients, not only under-16s. Find bulk-billing practices through Healthdirect or health.gov.au/bulkbilling.
- **Vision:** the ACT's public school vision check is in the Kindergarten Health Check; there is no routine public vision screening at ages 10–15 found. Optometrist eye tests can be Medicare bulk-billed. Low-income families may get the ACT Spectacles Subsidy (up to $200 every two years per eligible person).
- **Specsavers (commercial) checked:** $39 complete glasses (selected frames, single-vision, in-store only) ✅; eye tests bulk billed with a valid Medicare card ✅; hearing: a **free 15-minute hearing check**, but a full hearing assessment is **$49** unless eligible for the Hearing Services Program ⚠️ — so "free hearing tests" is only partly accurate.
- **Correction to the brief:** free Year 7 catch-up doses are available up to the **20th** birthday for dTpa (and Year 10 MenACWY) and the **26th** for HPV, not "up to 19".
